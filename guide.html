Quick start

Brent Studio is a video editor that runs on your device. Your videos, photos and projects are not uploaded anywhere. Here is the fastest way to a finished video:

  1. Tap Add and choose Videos, photos or music… (or tap Add media on the empty screen). Pick your clips.
  2. Your clips land on the timeline at the bottom, in the order you picked them. Press Play to watch.
  3. Tap a clip to select it. Trim, split, slow down or style it (see the next sections).
  4. Add a title, captions or music if you want them.
  5. Tap Export, choose a size, and save the finished video to your gallery or a folder.

Tip. Everything you do can be undone with Undo (Ctrl+Z on a keyboard). Your work is saved automatically while you edit, and you can also save a project file from Project → Save project file.

Getting around the screen

The screen has four main areas.

  • Top bar: the Project menu, the project name, the Add menu and the Export button.
  • Preview: shows your video. Use Play, or drag the playhead on the timeline to scrub.
  • Timeline: your clips in order. The video track is on top; titles, captions and music tracks sit below it. Pinch or use the zoom control to see more or less time.
  • Inspector: the panel for whatever is selected (a clip, a title, a music track). On a phone it opens below the preview. Sections open and close when you tap their headings.

On a phone, scroll the timeline sideways to move through your edit. Tap a clip once to select it, tap empty space to deselect.

Video shape (for YouTube, Shorts and more): the app picks the shape from your first clip, so a phone video starts out vertical. To change it at any time, tap the shape button next to the preview (it shows 9:16, 16:9 and so on) and pick a shape: Widescreen 16:9 (YouTube), Vertical 9:16 (Shorts, TikTok, Reels, Stories), Square 1:1, Feed portrait 4:5 (Instagram, Facebook), Tall portrait 3:4, Tall 2:3 (Pinterest), Classic 4:3 or Cinematic 21:9. Match my first clip goes back to your clip's own shape. Switch on Show the whole picture if you don't want the edges cropped. The Export preset list has matching social sizes.

Adding videos, photos and music

There are several ways to add things, so use whichever is closest to your thumb.

  • Add → Videos, photos or music… adds media to the end of the video track.
  • + Video in the timeline toolbar, the + on the video track header, and the Add video box after the last clip all open the same picker, so you never have to scroll back to the top.
  • Add → Overlay or logo on top… places a picture or clip above your video, for logos and picture-in-picture.
  • To crop an overlay any way you like, select it and tap Crop on the preview in the inspector. Drag any edge or corner on the preview, or drag inside the box to move it. The part you keep stays where it is. Tap Done when finished.
  • The Image button above the timeline puts a photo or logo on top of the video at the playhead. Duplicate copies the selected video, photo, title or sound right after it. Copy and Paste put it at the playhead instead (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+D also work on a keyboard).
  • Add → Music or sound… adds an audio track.
  • Add → Record voice-over… records your voice from the microphone while the video plays.
  • Add → Color background and Add → Animated background… add a plain or moving background clip (see Animated backgrounds).

Photos get a default length of a few seconds. You can change it in the inspector.

Editing on the timeline

Select a clip, then use the tools in the timeline toolbar.

  • Split: cuts the selected clip at the playhead (key S).
  • Trim start / Trim end: removes everything before or after the playhead (keys Q and W). You can also drag the edges of a clip.
  • Delete: removes the selected clip (key Delete). The gap closes up automatically.
  • Reorder: press and hold a clip, then drag it to a new place.
  • Markers: drop a marker at the playhead with M to remember a spot.
  • In and Out: set a range with I and O, and clear it with X. The range is used for exporting only that part and for some tools.

Tip. Keyboard fans: Space or K plays and pauses, J and L shuttle backwards and forwards, F fits the whole edit on screen, and \ (backslash) held down compares against the original.

Slow motion and fast forward

Open the Speed menu in the timeline toolbar. It does slow motion and fast forward.

  • Slow-mo moment at the playhead (2 s at 0.5×): splits the clip around the playhead and plays a two-second piece at half speed. Great for a jump or a goal.
  • Fast-forward moment at the playhead (4 s at 4×): plays four seconds around the playhead in one second. Great for a long walk, a drive or setting up.
  • Whole clip: ultra slow (0.125×, eight times slower), super slow (0.25×), slow (0.5×), a little slower (0.75×), a little faster (1.5×), fast (2×), super fast (4×), or time-lapse (8×, 16×, 32× and 64×).
  • Back to normal speed: returns the clip to 1×.
  • Any speed in between: select the clip and use Speed → Custom speed in the panel (0.125× to 64×).

Slowing a clip makes it longer; speeding it up makes it shorter, and the rest of the edit moves with it. If slow motion looks choppy, use a clip that was filmed at a higher frame rate (60 fps or more). Sound changes pitch at other speeds (at 8× and 16× it is mostly noise, and above 16× the clip's own sound is left out), so mute the clip under Sound or add music instead. At 32× and 64× the preview may update in small jumps on slower devices; the exported video is drawn frame by frame.

Titles and captions

  1. Move the playhead to where the text should appear and press T (or use the title button in the toolbar).
  2. Type your text in the inspector. Choose a style, font, size, color and position.
  3. Want a second line, like a name, role, date or tagline? Type it in the Subtitle box. The Subtitle style section sets its font, size, color, and whether it sits above or below the title.
  4. Drag the ends of the title on its track to change how long it stays.

Press C to add a caption in the same way. Captions are meant for spoken words and have their own looks.

Automatic captions listen to your video and type the words for you. Open the Tools menu and choose Automatic captions…. The first time, the speech model downloads once (it needs internet, then works on the device). Always read the result and fix mistakes before you export.

You can also Import captions (SRT/VTT) you already have, or Save captions as SRT to use elsewhere.

Styles such as Title + tagline, Chapter, Name & role, Quote, Episode, Event, Vlog and Breaking news come with a subtitle already filled in. Just type over it, or clear the box to hide it.

Pro motion graphics: Liquid blob, Liquid drip and Liquid fill pour and drain the text, Cartoon card and Cartoon burst spring in on a bouncy card, and Pop-up book, Paper cutout and Stop-motion build the title from paper pieces. You can also pick these entrances and exits under Animation → Comes in / Goes out.

Tip. Pro title and caption styles are free to try in the preview. Exporting them needs Pro.

End credits

  1. Choose Add → End credits…. The credits go after your last clip.
  2. Type a Heading (for example Thanks for watching), your Names, and a Closing line.
  3. Write one credit per line as Role: Name. A line that ends with a colon, like Cast:, becomes a section heading. An empty line starts a new page.
  4. Pick a Credits style, then choose how the names move and how the video gives way to them.

AI voice-over

Turn written text into a spoken voice, on your device. Open Add → AI voice-over from text….

  1. Type or paste your script. Long scripts are fine.
  2. Choose a voice and a speed.
  3. Tap the preview button to listen first. Try other voices until you like one, before anything is added to your video.
  4. Set where it should begin with Starts at. Use the ±1 s nudge buttons or Start of video. By default it begins at the playhead.
  5. Tap Add to put the voice on a music track. It is a normal audio clip, so you can drag it to a new place, trim it or change its volume.

The voice model downloads the first time you use it. AI voices are synthetic: when you share a video that uses one, say that the voice is AI-generated. The rules that come with the voice model are listed in the AI voice dialog and in the Terms of use.

Music, sound and volume

  • Select a music track to set its volume, fade in and fade out in the inspector.
  • Starts at sets exactly where the track begins. Use the ±1 s buttons to nudge it, or Start of video to snap it to the beginning.
  • Drag a track left or right on its row to move it.
  • Remove silences… in the Tools menu cuts the quiet gaps from a clip.
  • Layered audio (two audio clips playing at the same time) is a Pro feature.

Only use music you have the right to use. Platforms can mute or remove videos that use music you do not own.

Color and looks

Select a clip and open the color and look sections of the inspector. You can adjust brightness, contrast and color, or pick a ready-made look. Compare with the original at any time by holding \. Color grading, looks and LUTs are Pro features.

Brand kit… in the Project menu stores your logo, colors and fonts so intros, outros and titles match every time. Brand intro at start and Brand outro at end in the Add menu use them.

Transitions, photo motion and 3D photos

A transition is the change between two clips. Select the later clip and choose a transition in the inspector, then set its length. Besides the classics there are Flash, Spin, Whip and Glitch.

Photos can move so they feel alive. Select a photo and pick a motion: zoom in or out, pan left, right, up or down, Drift, or 3D.

3D gives the photo real depth, like a gentle camera move through the scene. The app works out what is near and far on your device. The first time it downloads a small model; if that is not possible it still makes a simple depth effect. 3D uses one of your daily AI uses on the free plan.

Hand-swipe transition: film shot 1 and end it by swiping your hand toward the lens. Start shot 2 with your hand covering the lens and pull it away. Put both clips next to each other, cut each one where the hand covers the lens, then tap the gap between them and choose Hand swipe or Shake. Speed on each clip (for example 1.5× near the swipe) makes it snappier.

Animated backgrounds

Moving backgrounds for titles, intros, quotes and text posts. They need no photos and no downloads.

  1. Choose Add → Animated background….
  2. Pick a style (for example Flow, Aurora, Bokeh, Stars, Waves, Grid, Confetti, Snow, Rays, Plasma, Embers or Shapes). The previews are moving.
  3. Choose a color palette and a speed, then tap Add.
  4. Select the clip at any time to change the style, colors, speed or length.

Put a title on a track above it to make an instant intro. Some styles are Pro; the picker marks them.

Photos on top of the video: frames and collage

Put photos (or short clips) on top of your video like printed photos, the way travel edits do.

  1. Move the playhead to where the photos should start.
  2. Choose Add → Photo collage on top… and tap Pick photos….
  3. Choose a Layout: Cascade (photos land in different spots and overlap), Grid 2×2 (split screen), Row of 3, Scrapbook (tilted prints) or One by one.
  4. Choose a Frame: Border, Rounded, Shadow card, Instant photo (with a handwritten caption), Film strip or Taped photo.
  5. Choose how each one comes in and how often a new photo appears, then tap Add on top of the video.

Each photo is a normal overlay. Select it to change its frame, frame color, position, size or motion under Photo frame & motion. Basic motion (fade, pop, slide, zoom) is free; whip, drop and bounce, spin, flip and swing are Pro.

Title templates (Add → Title templates…) place a few titles that arrive one after another, like “And with that, the 2026 season comes to an end”. Each part is a normal title you can edit, and Placement → Move left / right / up / down moves any title anywhere.

Masks and blending (new sky, moon, double exposure)

Select an overlay and open Mask & blend.

  • Sky replace: put a sky or planet picture on the overlay track and tap Sky replace: show above a line. Move the line to your horizon with Position up / down and Angle, and soften it with Soft edge.
  • Mask shapes: Split line, Band, Circle and Rectangle. Invert shows the other side.
  • Hide black background: for a moon, planet, fire or light leak on black. It uses the Screen blend.
  • Hide white background: for drawings or logos on white. It uses the Multiply blend.
  • Blend modes: Screen, Multiply, Overlay, Soft light, Hard light, Lighten, Darken, Add, Color dodge, Color burn and Difference.

Tip. Finish the look with a color look on the main clip so the new sky and the video match.

Overlay layers: stack videos, photos and animated backgrounds

The overlay track has numbered layers: 3 shown on a computer (up to 5), 2 on a phone (up to 4). Higher layers are drawn on top. Something added where a layer is already busy goes to the next free layer automatically.

  1. Tap Add → Animated background…, choose Its own layer (under overlays) and Add at the playhead. It goes on layer 1.
  2. Add your video on top: tap the + on the Overlay row of the timeline (or the stacked-layers button next to a clip in your media), then pick it. It goes on layer 2, above the background. (Dragging from the media list only works with a mouse; on a tablet or phone use the + button.)
  3. Select it and set Effects → Background behind people → Remove. The person now stands on the animated background.

Change an overlay's layer by dragging it up or down on the timeline, or with Overlay layer in its Timing panel. A clip already on the main video can go onto a layer with Move to an overlay layer.

Remove background and text behind a person

The app finds the people in a video or photo on your device. Nothing is uploaded. These are AI features: a few free uses a day, unlimited with Pro.

Remove the background: select the clip, open Effects and set Background behind people to Remove (or Blur to keep the place but soften it).

  1. To put a person on a new background, add the new background (video, photo or Add → Color background) to the main video track.
  2. Add the clip with the person as an overlay (Add → Overlay or logo on top…) and stretch it to the same length.
  3. Select the overlay and set Background behind people to Remove. Move and size it under Position.
  • Keep one person only: tap it, then tap the person you want. The app follows that person as they move, and drops other people in the shot.
  • Fix with brush: paint with Keep over things the AI misses (a scooter, a bag, a pet) and with Remove over things it kept by mistake. Red shows what stays. Tap Done when finished. Brush strokes stay where you paint them, so they suit steady shots.
  • A shot with no person in it keeps its background, so you never get a black screen.

Text behind a person:

  1. Film yourself standing still for a moment with a plain wall behind you, and add the clip to the main video track.
  2. Tap Add → Title templates… → Text behind person, or add a normal title.
  3. Type your words, make them big, and put them at head or chest height with Placement → Move up / down.
  4. In Placement, turn on Put the text behind the person. The person is drawn in front of the letters.

Tip. Works best with one person, a clear background and even light. Give the app a moment the first time while it finds the person. For a glitchy look like popular edits, pick the Glitch entrance and add a Glitch transition.

Text behind mountains or buildings

  1. Use a steady shot (on a tripod, or with the phone held still) with sky above the mountains, trees or buildings.
  2. Tap Add → Title templates… → Text behind mountains. The app finds where the sky ends.
  3. Select the title. Under Placement, tap Edit the area to fix spots: drag to paint what should be in front of the text, switch to Erase to take parts away, then tap Done.
  4. Choose the Rise from behind entrance so the words come up from behind the mountains. Soft edge blends the outline.

Any title can do this: turn on Put the text behind something in Placement and choose An area I choose. It's free and doesn't use the AI allowance.

Lyric videos and cutting to the beat

Add your song first (Add → Music or sound…). Select it and open Beat:

  • Mark the beats puts a marker on every beat (or every 2 beats, or every bar) so clip edges snap to them.
  • Cut clips to the beat trims every video clip so each cut lands on a beat. Put your clips on the video track first.
  • Lyrics on the beat… opens the lyrics tool.

Lyrics, one word at a time: tap Add → Lyrics, word by word… and paste the words. Choose the timing:

  • On the beat: a new word every beat, 2 beats or bar, starting at the playhead.
  • Tap along: the edit plays and you tap the big button (or press Space) each time a word is sung. Use this for singing that doesn't follow the beat.
  • Evenly: the same time for every word.

Pick a look (Big and bold, Clean, Movie, or Behind the person). Every word is a normal title you can retype, restyle or move. Adding lyrics again replaces the previous ones.

Side-by-side strips and top-to-bottom transitions

  1. Tap Add → Photo collage on top… and pick 2 to 8 photos or clips.
  2. Choose the layout Side-by-side strips (tall strips next to each other) or Stacked rows (bands from top to bottom).
  3. Choose Slide up or Slide down as the way each one comes in, and set A new photo every to about 0.1–0.2 seconds for a fast reveal.

For the next shot coming down from the top, tap the gap between two clips and choose Slide down (top to bottom) or Push down. Add → Title templates… → Location card gives the travel-recap title with small spaced words above and below a big place name.

High quality AI on a powerful PC

On a desktop or laptop with a strong graphics card, open Project settings and turn on High quality AI (powerful PCs). The switch only appears on computers that can run it smoothly, never on phones or tablets.

  • Cut-out: Remove background finds the main subject, not just people (a child and their scooter, a pet, a car), with cleaner edges. Keep one person only and the brush still work on top of it.
  • Captions: a Best (powerful PC) accuracy setting with a much larger speech model.

Each model downloads once the first time you use it (a few hundred MB), then works offline. If this computer turns out too slow, the app tells you and you can switch it off.

Exporting and saving your video

  1. Tap Export.
  2. Choose a size (720p, 1080p, or higher with Pro) and a quality level.
  3. Tap the export button and keep the app open until it finishes. A progress bar shows how far along it is.
  4. When it is done, choose Save to gallery (the video appears in your Photos or Gallery app) or Save to a folder to pick where it goes.

You get a message and a notification when the video is saved, so you can leave the app while it works. If a notification does not appear, check that notifications are allowed for Brent Studio in your phone settings.

Tip. Longer videos and 4K take longer and warm up the device. Charge your phone, keep it cool, and close other apps for the best results.

Projects and autosave

  • New project starts fresh. Your previous edit is autosaved first.
  • Save project file writes a small project file you can keep or move. It does not contain your videos, only how they are arranged.
  • Open project file… loads one. If the app cannot find a video, use Find missing media… and point it to the originals.
  • Files are matched by name, or by size when the name has changed. If a file still doesn't match, tap Choose… next to it to link it by hand.
  • On Android, Find missing media → Find on this device searches the tablet, its memory cards and any plugged-in USB drive or SSD for all the missing files at once; tap Link to put them back. If some are on a drive it can't see, tap Search a folder or drive and choose the drive once; it is searched every time after that.
  • On Android, big videos (over 150 MB) aren't copied into the app, so they don't fill your tablet. The app keeps access to them instead. After a restart, plug in the USB drive, SSD or memory card they're on and tap Reconnect.
  • Autosave keeps your work if the app closes or the phone restarts. Do not delete the original videos from your phone while you still need the project.

Your account and syncing projects

Signing in is optional. Sign in with your Google account (the Sign in button at the top, or Project → Account and sync) to use the same Pro and the same projects on your phone, tablet and PC.

  • Pro follows you: Pro bought on your phone (Google Play) or added to your account works on up to 3 of your devices. Signing in on a 4th signs out the one used longest ago.
  • Projects sync through your own Google Drive: after each change the project is saved to Brent Studio's private app folder in your Drive. The app can't see your other Drive files.
  • Open a project on another device: Project → Your projects in Google Drive… and tap Open. If a newer version was saved on another device, the app offers to open it.
  • Videos and photos: they stay on each device unless you turn on Keep this project's videos and photos in Drive in your account panel. Then the other device downloads them when you open the project. This uses your Drive space; big videos take a while on mobile data.
  • Edited on two devices at once? Nothing is lost: the second version is saved as its own project with the device name added.

Tip. Delete your account any time from the account panel. Purchases stay with Google Play or the Microsoft Store, and projects in your Drive stay yours until you delete them.

The Windows app

On a Windows PC, the Brent Studio app uses your computer's full power: more memory than a browser tab, your graphics card, and FFmpeg.

  • Any video format: videos the editor can't read directly (HEVC/H.265 without the Windows extension, ProRes, AVI, MXF, WMV and more) are converted when you add them, on your graphics card when it can. Converted copies are kept so they open instantly next time.
  • Export on the graphics card: in Export, choose Encoder → Graphics card (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD or Intel Quick Sync) and the format: H.264, H.265 or AV1. The video is saved straight to the folder you pick; Show in folder opens it.
  • Saving: projects, pictures and sound are saved straight to a folder you choose.
  • Pro on Windows: buy it in the app with the Microsoft Store's own checkout, or sign in with the Google account you used on your phone.

Tip. H.264 on graphics cards goes up to 4096 pixels wide. For wider videos (cinematic 4K or 8K), choose H.265 or AV1.

Speed depends on your device

Brent Studio does its work on your own phone or tablet, not on a server. That keeps your videos private, but it also means how fast it is depends on your device: its processor, graphics and video encoder, free memory (RAM) and storage, how warm it is, and what else is running.

This matters most for AI voice-over, automatic captions, 3D photos, background removal, exporting in 4K, and long or busy edits. The same project can finish quickly on one device and take much longer, stutter or fail on another, even between devices of a similar age or price. A newer phone with plenty of free memory is usually faster than an older, entry-level phone or some tablets. We can't promise a particular speed or result on any device.

  • Close other apps before a big job, and keep the device charged and cool.
  • Free up storage. Exports and AI models need room.
  • Turn off battery saver while exporting or using AI features.
  • Export at 1080p first. Try 4K only on a device that handles it comfortably.
  • For AI voice-over, make shorter scripts in parts. For captions, choose Faster instead of Better.
  • The first use of an AI feature also downloads its model, so use Wi-Fi.

Tips for phones and tablets

  • Use + Video next to the timeline instead of scrolling to the top.
  • Turn the phone sideways for a bigger preview and timeline.
  • Zoom the timeline in for exact cuts, and zoom out to rearrange.
  • Edit with the phone plugged in for long projects or 4K.
  • Keep some storage free: exports need space equal to the finished video, plus extra while working.

Troubleshooting

  • A video will not import: the file type may not be supported by your phone. Try another clip, or convert it to MP4 (H.264).
  • Export stops or fails: free up storage, close other apps and try a lower resolution first.
  • The video is saved but I cannot find it: open your Gallery or Photos app and look in the Movies or Brent Studio album, or use Save to a folder and choose a place you can find.
  • No sound in the export: check that the clip or music is not muted and the volume is up in the inspector.
  • AI captions or voice do not start: the first use needs internet to download the model. Check your connection and try again.
  • The app feels slow, or AI voice or an export takes very long: speed depends on your device's processor and free memory (see Speed depends on your device). Try a smaller export size, shorter scripts, close other apps, and let the device cool down.

Still stuck? Email the support address shown on the app’s Google Play page and say what you were doing and what you saw.
