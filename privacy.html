Brent Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026. Brent Studio is made by Bret Michael Dumaraos ("I", "me"). Contact: bretmdumaraos@gmail.com.

Summary

Brent Studio is a video editor that runs on your device. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

Your videos, photos, sounds and projects

Everything you import, edit and export is processed and stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. None of it is uploaded to me or to anyone else. Exports are saved where you choose with Android's Save screen. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes the app's copies.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you record a voice-over. The recording is made and kept on your device.

Notifications

On Android 13 and newer the app asks if it may show notifications. It uses them only to tell you when a video or project has been saved. No notification content leaves your device, and you can say no or turn them off in Settings.

AI features

Automatic captions, AI voice-over and background removal run on your device. Your audio, video and scripts are never sent anywhere. The first time you use captions or AI voice-over, the app downloads the speech library from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and the speech and voice models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). To count the free plan's daily AI uses, the app stores a small counter on your device.

Other network requests

The app loads title fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details in order to deliver the files; their own privacy policies apply (Google, Hugging Face, jsDelivr). I do not receive this information.

Subscriptions

Brent Studio Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; I never see your card or bank details. The app asks Google Play on your device whether your subscription is active. Google shares limited order details with me through Play Console (such as order number, date, product, price and country) so I can handle refunds and support requests. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

Because your data stays on your device, you control it: delete projects in the app, uninstall the app, or clear its storage at any time. You can cancel a subscription in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
