How Brent Studio handles your videos, projects, optional Google sign-in, Google Drive sync and account data.

Summary

Brent Studio is a video editor that runs on your device. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and I don't sell anyone's data. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, the app keeps a small account (your email address and your signed-in devices) so Pro and your projects follow you to your other devices. Your projects sync through your own Google Drive, not through me.

Your videos, photos, sounds and projects

Everything you import, edit and export is processed on your device and stored in the app's own storage. Exports are saved where you choose. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes the app's copies. If you turn on project sync (below), copies also go to your own Google Drive. They are never uploaded to me.

Signing in with Google (optional)

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google asks you to allow Brent Studio to see your email address and to use its own private app folder in your Google Drive. The app can't see, change or delete any of your other Drive files.

To keep your account, my account service stores: your email address; your Google account ID; a list of your signed-in devices (a random ID the app makes, a name like "Android phone" or "Windows PC", and when each was last used); and which Pro purchases or grants belong to your account, with their end dates. An account can be signed in on 3 devices at a time. The account service runs on Cloudflare (cloudflare.com), which hosts it for me and handles the requests; Cloudflare's privacy policy applies to that hosting.

Project sync with Google Drive

When you're signed in, your projects (the edit itself: cuts, titles, effects and settings) are saved to Brent Studio's private app folder in your Google Drive after you make changes, so you can open them on your other devices. Videos, photos and sounds are uploaded only for projects where you turn on "Keep this project's videos and photos in Drive". These files go directly between your device and Google; they don't pass through my account service and I can't see them. They use your Drive storage. You can turn sync off, delete projects from Drive in the app (Project > Your projects in Google Drive), or remove all of Brent Studio's Drive data in Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps.

Brent Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sign you in and to sync your own projects, never for ads and never shared with anyone else.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you record a voice-over. The recording is made and kept on your device.

Notifications

On Android 13 and newer the app asks if it may show notifications. It uses them only to tell you when a video or project has been saved. No notification content leaves your device, and you can say no or turn them off in Settings.

AI features

Automatic captions, AI voice-over and background removal run on your device. Your audio, video and scripts are never sent anywhere. The first time you use captions, AI voice-over or the high quality AI on a powerful PC, the app downloads the libraries from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and the models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). To count the free plan's daily AI uses, the app stores a small counter on your device.

Windows app

The Windows version works the same way and keeps your files on your PC. It uses FFmpeg on your PC to open more video formats and to export with your graphics card; this happens on your PC only. Converted copies are kept in the app's folder on your PC so they open faster next time. When you sign in with Google on Windows, the sign-in opens in your web browser and the app keeps the sign-in key encrypted with Windows' own protection.

Other network requests

The app loads title fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address and basic browser details in order to deliver the files; their own privacy policies apply (Google, Hugging Face, jsDelivr). I do not receive this information.

Purchases

On Android, Brent Studio Pro is sold through Google Play; on Windows, through the Microsoft Store. Google or Microsoft processes the payment; I never see your card or bank details. The app asks the store on your device whether your purchase is active. If you're signed in on Android, the app also sends your Google Play purchase token to my account service, which asks Google Play whether the purchase is real and active, so Pro also works on your other devices. Google and Microsoft share limited order details with me (such as order number, date, product, price and country) so I can handle refunds and support requests. See Google's and Microsoft's privacy statements.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open your account (the round button with your initial, or Project > Account and sync) and choose Delete account. This deletes your email address, devices and account Pro from my account service right away. If you can't open the app, email bretmdumaraos@gmail.com from the email address of your account and I'll delete it within 30 days. Purchases stay with Google Play or the Microsoft Store. Projects in your Google Drive stay yours until you delete them (see Project sync above). Server logs kept by Cloudflare for security are deleted on their normal schedule.

Your choices

Signing in and sync are optional: the app works fully without them. You can sign out, turn sync off, delete projects, uninstall the app or clear its storage at any time. Cancel a subscription in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or at account.microsoft.com > Services & subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: bretmdumaraos@gmail.com.
