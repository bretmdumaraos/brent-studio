6. Performance depends on your device

Brent Studio does its work on your own phone or tablet, not on a server. So how fast it is, and whether some jobs work at all, depends on your device: its processor, graphics chip and video encoder, free memory (RAM) and storage, its temperature and battery saver, and what else is running. This matters most for AI voice-over, automatic captions, 3D photos, background removal, exporting at high resolutions such as 4K, and long or complex edits.

The same project can finish quickly on one device and run slowly, stutter or fail on another, even between devices of a similar age or price. I can't promise any particular speed, resolution, length or result on any device, and I'm not responsible for the time a job takes or for jobs that a device is too slow or has too little memory to finish. If something is slow, close other apps, keep the device charged and cool, free up storage, and try a smaller export size, shorter scripts or fewer effects.
